## Your task

Humans learn best through counter-intuitive lessons (hare beats tortoise, David beats Goliath, less is more). What is the **hot take, counter-intuitive lesson** in this paper — the finding that goes against what most people would expect, or that flips a common assumption on its head?

Write a single punchy paragraph (2-4 sentences) capturing this counter-intuitive insight. No headers, no bullets, no preamble. Just the takeaway. Use plain, direct language — no "this study suggests" hedging. State the surprising thing clearly and explain why it works.

## Examples (format reference)

### Example 1
Giving the AI less — a single razor-sharp sentence that sums up each persona — beats flooding it with rich back-stories, because the model then focuses on the few traits that actually move decisions instead of getting lost in narrative noise.

### Example 2
The quickest way to make an AI audience more balanced isn't to add more data or "randomize" your sampling — it's to do the opposite and deliberately seed it with an extreme minority cue (e.g., "right-authoritarian"). Two words of prompt overpower billions of learned tokens, swinging the group back toward the center faster than any amount of fine-tuning or bigger samples.

### Example 3
While elaborate human-like personas help LLMs role-play creatively in simulation, simpler structured personas are actually more effective for task-specific real-world performance — the opposite of what every persona-engineering tutorial recommends.

### Example 4
The messier you let the model be — splitting it into hundreds of quirky, randomly sampled personas instead of one carefully tuned "ideal" respondent — the closer its answers land to real-world humans.

### Example 5
You get far closer to real-world opinions by giving your AI personas one or two mindset clues ("How much do you talk about this?" "How important is it to you?") than by feeding them pages of demographic detail — meaning attitude signals, not age or income, are the secret sauce for realistic synthetic surveys.
